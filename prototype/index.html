Step 02 · Settings

How should we score and slice it?

The defaults suit most clients. Keep them unchanged between recurring runs of the same client so results stay comparable.

Analysis window

Recency is counted up to the snapshot date; the day itself is not included.
All amounts are converted with the monthly FX table.

Scoring

R and M use quintiles; F uses fixed bins because most customers buy once or twice.
Means 1 · 2 · 3 · 4–5 · 6+
Freeze them to track how customers move between segments over time.

Outliers and B2B

Customers far above the bulk — usually B2B, staff or test accounts.

Cohorts

Groups customers by when they first bought and follows them over time.
First-ever needs first_purchase_date in the customers table, so returning customers don't look new.

Segment rules

Checked top to bottom — the first match wins. Use R, F, M (1–5), recency_days, frequency, monetary, has_prior_history.
